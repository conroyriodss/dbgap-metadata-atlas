# Published reference studies: replication versus analogy

The code's literature-method companions are not claims of numerical replication. The original
administrative microdata, source-specific units and follow-up windows are not bundled.

**Paltoo et al. (2014), Data use under the NIH GWAS Data Sharing Policy and future directions.**
DOI: `10.1038/ng.3062`; [primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4182942/).
The adopted module distinguishes sample-level phenotype/assay composition (Figure 1),
consent-group applications/approval/download/turnaround (Figure 2), institution categories
(Table 1), and DAC-reported secondary-use publications (Figure 3). Current root-level modalities,
root-WLID relationships and study-page literature are descriptive analogues unless matching
records are supplied. Historical totals are not interpolated into invented annual series.

**Coady et al. (2017), Use of the National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Data Repository.**
DOI: `10.1056/NEJMsa1603542`; [primary article](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMsa1603542).
The module implements availability/request panels, supplied-location summaries and
availability-to-request-associated-publication Kaplan-Meier analysis where suitable histories
exist. It retains censored studies. A coordinate plot is not an exact reproduction of the original
map. InCites percentile analyses require that metric; RCR is not substituted. The supplemental
Poisson specification is not represented as replicated.

**Cabili et al. (2021), Empirical validation of an automated approach to data use oversight.**
DOI: `10.1016/j.xgen.2021.100031`; [primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9903839/).
Use this as a definitions comparator for datasets, releases, PI requesters, collaborators and
administrative records, not an interchangeable denominator for every current record count.

These references were inherited from the reviewed project sources. Their content is not newly
reproduced by repository packaging. No paper figures or full text are redistributed here. Primary
source verification is a prerequisite before adding a new bibliographic or numerical assertion.
